Good questions.

Is Nexora free?

Yes. No subscription and no account.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Nexora is shared directly as an APK, so Android may ask you to allow the install once. Updates then come through Check for updates in the app.

Are there ads?

Nexora has no ads of its own. Videos come from third-party players, and some of them show ads. The app blocks their pop-ups and redirects.

Will every title have Hindi?

Not always. It depends on the title and the server. If one server doesn't have it, try the next.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Nexora is for Android 7.0 and newer.

What do you collect?

An anonymous device count, version, country and what is played, so we know what people use. No names, no accounts, and no saved IP addresses.
